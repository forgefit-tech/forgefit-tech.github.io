Why Forgefit

Built different.

Every feature exists because real athletes asked for it. Here's what makes Forgefit worth your time.

📅

Flexible Scheduling

Life happens. Missed workouts automatically queue forward to the next open day — no pile-up, no skipped sessions, no guilt.

Key Differentiator
🧠

Smart Pre-fill

Every time you start a workout, your last logged weights and reps are automatically loaded. Zero re-entry, maximum focus.

📓

Contextual Notes

Attach notes to workouts, exercises, plans, or anything else. Tag by context — nutrition, recovery, technique — and search them instantly.

Key Differentiator
📈

Plan History Timeline

See every plan you've ever run, when you ran it, and how far you got. Replay any plan with one tap.

Key Differentiator
⏱️

Auto Rest Timer

The rest countdown starts the moment you mark a set complete. No tapping, no forgetting — just the right amount of rest, every time.

🏆

Personal Records

PRs are tracked automatically per exercise. View your progression chart, celebrate milestones, and chase new records every session.

🔄

Draft Persistence

Accidentally close the app mid-workout? Your session is auto-saved every 10 seconds. Pick up exactly where you left off.

💪

Cardio + Strength

Log timed cardio exercises with a built-in countdown timer alongside your strength sets — all in one unified workout.

🗓️

Progress Calendar

See completed workouts and upcoming scheduled sessions on a single calendar. Tap any day to view details or start a scheduled workout.

Alpha Perk

Pro is completely free during alpha.

Every alpha tester gets full Pro access — all features, no limits, no payment info required. Ever. This is our thank-you for helping shape the product.
