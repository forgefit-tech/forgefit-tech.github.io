Features

Everything you need to train with purpose

From beginner to advanced, Forgefit gives you the tools to build a stronger, more consistent training practice.

Curated Training Plans

Choose from a library of expert-designed programs — powerlifting, hypertrophy, beginner strength, and more — or build your own from scratch.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Set your workout days, mark rest days, and let Forgefit build a rolling 7-day schedule that always keeps you on track — even when life gets in the way.

Lift Tracking & History

Log every set and rep, track your personal records, and visualize your progress over time with detailed charts and lift history.

AI Workout Coach

Get real-time guidance, form tips, and personalized recommendations from your built-in AI trainer — available anytime, right in the app.
